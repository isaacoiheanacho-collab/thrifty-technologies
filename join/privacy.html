Data Governance

Platform Privacy Policy

How personal details and ERP activity records are processed across community partner circles.

1. Data Collected for ERP Accounting

We process member registration data (full name, E.164 phone number) alongside transaction receipt entries to maintain clear, auditable ERP application records for each savings circle.

2. Purpose & Processing

Personal data is used exclusively to verify identity, confirm circle contributions, and allow Community Partner Admins to validate circle payouts accurately.

3. Data Access & Protection

Platform records are never commercialized or shared with third-party advertisers. All records are accessible to your verified Community Partner Admin for community bookkeeping.
