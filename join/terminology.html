Platform Glossary

Platform Terminology

Standardized concepts and official nomenclature used across the ROSCA Thrift & Savings ERP application.

Community Partner

The local church group, association, or organization managing the circle's co-savers on the platform.

Community Partner Admin

The verified coordinator managing circle allocations, validating member receipts, and supervising monthly payouts.

ERP Application

The core digital accounting system that logs every contribution, verifies receipt uploads, and maintains member records.

Time-Value Contribution (TVC)

A 2% annual adjustment rate that balances early payout convenience with late slot patience across the 12-month tenure.

Platform Maintenance Fee

A standard 1.5% fee applies to each payout to cover the server, database, hosting, and third-party service costs that keep the platform running.

Co-Saver / Member

A verified participant belonging to a 12-member savings circle called tenant.

ROSCA Thrift & Savings

The digital ERP application for church community savings circles. Operated by Thrifty Technologies, United Kingdom.
