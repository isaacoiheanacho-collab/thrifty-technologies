11. Time value collection (TVC)

11.1. The Platform incorporates a Time Value Collection adjustment ("TVC") into the calculation of Savings Circle Payouts.

11.2. TVC is intended to recognise the difference in collection timing between Co-Savers occupying different Collection Positions in a Circle.

11.3. The annual TVC rate is 2% per year.

11.4. The monthly rate used in the calculation is:

r = 0.02 / 12

11.5. The TVC calculation uses the number of Co-Savers in the Circle, the monthly Contribution amount, the Co-Saver's Collection Position and the Circle's pivot position.

11.6. The formula is as follows:

A. Base Pool

P = N × M

B. Pivot Position

Pivot = (N + 1) / 2

C. TVC Adjustment

Adjustment = (Pivot − T) × M × r × N

D. Adjusted Payout

P_net = P + Adjustment

E. Equivalent Factored Formula

P_net = P × [1 + r × (Pivot − T)]

11.7. For this calculation:

  • N = total number of Co-Savers in the Circle;
  • M = monthly Contribution per Co-Saver;
  • T = the Co-Saver's Collection Position, numbered from 1 to N;
  • r = monthly TVC rate, calculated as the annual rate divided by 12;
  • P = base pool, calculated as N × M;
  • P_net = adjusted Payout after applying the TVC adjustment.

11.8. Where the Collection Position is earlier than the pivot position, the formula produces a positive adjustment. Where the Collection Position is later than the pivot position, the formula produces a negative adjustment.

11.9. A Collection Position equal to the pivot position produces no TVC adjustment.

11.10. The TVC is an adjustment in the distribution of the Circle's Payouts. It is not a separate fee charged by Thrifty.

11.11. Neither Thrifty nor the Community Admin separately receives, holds, collects or distributes TVC as a standalone payment. It is reflected through the Payout calculation and the corresponding allocation of the Circle pool.

11.12. Thrifty provides the software calculation and display of TVC. The Community Admin remains responsible for administering the Circle and ensuring that the calculation is applied in accordance with the agreed Circle Rules.

11.13. The TVC calculation must be disclosed to Co-Savers before they join a Circle.

11.14. Any change to the annual TVC rate or calculation method must be communicated to affected Co-Savers and agreed where required before being applied to an existing Circle.
