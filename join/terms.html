Platform Terms of Service

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. Governing the operation of the ROSCA Thrift & Savings digital ERP application.

1. Platform Role & ERP Infrastructure

Thrifty Technologies Ltd ("Operator", "we", "us") provides software infrastructure for Rotating Savings and Credit Associations (ROSCAs). We provide an electronic ERP application that records contributions, verifies receipts, and tracks payout distributions managed by Community Partner Admins.

2. Financial Terms & Maintenance

  • Platform Fee: A 1.5% platform maintenance fee is assessed on payouts to maintain system accounting and operational security.
  • Time-Value Contribution (TVC): A 2% annual TVC rate is applied across the 12-month tenure to reward later receivers and adjust early collection values fairly.

3. Community Partner Administration

Each savings circle operates under a designated Community Partner Admin who handles local member verification and circle onboarding. All platform-level legal conditions apply universally to all co-savers participating through `/join/index.html`.
