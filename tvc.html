Time-Value Collection (TVC)

TVC is Thrifty's position-based payout adjustment. It recognises that receiving a rotating savings pool earlier or later changes the timing benefit for each member. The adjustment is calculated from the individual contribution amount and the circle's actual membership—not from an annual rate or an arbitrary percentage of the pool.

Why TVC is used

A member collecting early receives the pooled contributions sooner than a member collecting later. TVC recognises this difference by adjusting the amount allocated to each collection position. Earlier positions receive a lower adjusted payout; later positions receive a higher adjusted payout.

The maximum adjustment in either direction is 50% of one individual contribution for the collection period. The adjustment is not an annual interest rate and is not calculated as a percentage of the total pool. The positive and negative adjustments balance across the circle.

How the calculation works

The calculation adapts to the actual number of co-savers and the contribution amount agreed for the circle.

1. Gross pool

Gross Pool = N × M

2. Pivot position

Pivot = (N + 1) / 2

3. TVC adjustment

TVC Adjustment = M × (T − Pivot) / (N − 1)

N is the number of co-savers, M is the individual contribution for one collection period, and T is the member's collection position numbered from 1 to N.

4. Adjusted payout before maintenance

Adjusted Payout = Gross Pool + TVC Adjustment

5. Platform maintenance fee and final payout

Maintenance Fee = Adjusted Payout × 1.5%
Final Payout = Adjusted Payout − Maintenance Fee

The maintenance fee is separate from TVC and is applied after the TVC adjustment.

Where the money goes

  • Co-savers transfer contributions directly to the bank account designated by their Community Partner or Community Admin.
  • The Community Admin administers the circle's pool and arranges the payout.
  • The Community Admin deducts the 1.5% platform maintenance fee from the adjusted payout and transfers that fee to Thrifty.
  • Thrifty provides the software, calculation and records. It does not receive, hold, pool, lend or distribute co-savers' contributions or payout funds.
