Time-Value Collection (TVC)

TVC adjusts each payout according to when a member receives the circle's pooled contributions. Members collecting earlier receive a lower adjusted payout; members waiting longer receive a higher adjusted payout. The adjustments balance across all 12 collection positions.

Why TVC is used

A member receiving the pool in the first month obtains access to the pooled contributions much earlier than a member receiving it in the twelfth month. TVC recognises this difference in collection timing through a transparent adjustment to the payout amounts. It is not a separate interest payment or a standalone fee collected by Thrifty.

The standard annual TVC rate is 9.0909090909% (1/11 per annum). The corresponding monthly rate used in the existing linear pivot calculation is 1/132, or approximately 0.75757576% per month.

How the calculation works

The standard circle has 12 members contributing £1,000 each month, giving a gross pool of £12,000 before TVC and the platform maintenance fee.

1. Gross pool

P = N × M = 12 × £1,000 = £12,000

2. Pivot position

Pivot = (N + 1) / 2 = (12 + 1) / 2 = 6.5

3. TVC adjustment

Adjustment = (T − Pivot) × M × r × N

Where T is the member's slot number, N is 12, M is £1,000 and r is the monthly rate 1/132.

4. Adjusted payout, before maintenance

Adjusted Payout = P + Adjustment

5. Platform maintenance fee and final payout

Maintenance Fee = Adjusted Payout × 1.5%
Final Payout = Adjusted Payout × 0.985

The maintenance fee is applied after TVC. Because it is a percentage of the adjusted payout, the fee is lower for an earlier, reduced payout and higher for a later, increased payout.

The TVC adjustments total zero across the 12 positions. The adjustment changes how the gross pool is allocated between collection positions; it is not an additional amount paid to Thrifty. The maintenance fee is separate and is calculated on each adjusted payout.

Where the money goes

  • Co-Savers pay contributions directly into the bank account designated by their Community Partner or Community Admin.
  • The Community Admin administers the circle's pool and arranges the payout.
  • The Community Admin deducts the 1.5% maintenance fee from the adjusted payout and transfers that fee to Thrifty.
  • Thrifty provides the software calculation and records. It does not receive, hold, pool, lend or distribute Co-Savers' contributions or payout funds.

These figures are an illustration of the standard, fully funded circle. A missed or unconfirmed contribution can affect the funds actually available for a payout. See the Terms of Service and the applicable community rules for the responsibilities and procedures that apply.
