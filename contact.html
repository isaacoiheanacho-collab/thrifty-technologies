Instant Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here are the most common inquiries from members and administrators.

Does Thrifty Technologies hold or process our money?

No. Thrifty Technologies operates pure software infrastructure. All money flows directly between co-savers and their designated community partner's local bank account. We provide the immutable ERP application, verification matching, and automated tracking—we never take custody of your funds.

What happens if a co-saver misses a contribution deadline?

Each community partner sets their own specific operational rules and grace periods. In the ERP application, the circle status will show pending receipts until verified by the community partner admin. Late contributions may affect future slot eligibility according to your group's agreement.

How is the Time-Value Collection (TVC) calculated?

TVC balances early collection benefits with late collection patience. It is calculated at a standard 2% per annum rate, scaled dynamically depending on your assigned slot position (from Slot 1 through Slot 12) within the rotation cycle.

How do I register as a community partner admin?

Click on the "Become a Community Partner" button in the top navigation or footer. Once registered, you will complete basic identity verification (KYC) with the Super Admin to activate your independent community environment.

How do co-savers log into their community page?

Co-savers do not use the main platform operator login dropdown. Instead, each community partner provides their co-savers with a dedicated community link where members log in directly using their verified phone number.
