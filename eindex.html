The mechanic

What a savings circle is

A ROSCA — Rotating Savings and Credit Association — is a savings tradition used across the world. A small group contributes a fixed amount every month. Each month, one member receives the whole pool. Over 12 months, everyone contributes the same total and receives the pool exactly once.

01 — Members

12 people, one circle

A circle is exactly 12 verified members. Every member contributes the same amount every month. After 12 months, everyone has contributed twelve times and received once.

02 — Payout

One collection per month

Each month, one member receives the whole pot. Slot numbers are assigned randomly — no favouritism, no negotiation. Slot 1 collects in month 1, slot 12 in month 12.

03 — Fairness

Fair to everyone

Early receivers pay a small time-value premium, later receivers receive a small bonus. The adjustment is 2% per year — the same principle that makes any savings scheme fair.

The platform

What we add

The ROSCA mechanic is ancient. What's missing is a proper ledger. On paper, disputes happen. On WhatsApp, receipts get lost. With cash, there's no audit trail. We fix that.

Verified members

Every member completes identity verification. Full legal name, address, bank details. No anonymous participants.

Referenced transfers

Contributions and payouts carry a unique reference so anyone can trace which transfer belongs to which cycle.

Append-only ledger

Once a transaction is recorded, it cannot be edited or deleted — by anyone, including the platform operator.

Shared visibility

All 12 members see the same contributions, receipts, and progress. No private balances.

Real-time notifications

Members get pushed updates when contributions are confirmed, when payouts happen, and when it's their turn.

We never touch money

Funds stay in your branch's own bank account. We reconcile what happened — we don't hold or move funds.

1.5%
on the pot, at payout time

No setup fee, no monthly subscription, no per-member charge. You only pay when money moves.

A digital ledger for community savings circles — built for church branches and other trusted groups who already save together.

The platform is operated by Thrifty Technologies, a United Kingdom entity. We provide the software, the servers, the ledger, and the support.

The branches — individual churches or community groups — are the ones running the actual savings circles. Each branch has its own bank account, its own pool of members, and its own admin who verifies contributions and payouts.

What the platform does — and does not do

✓ We do

  • Record each member's contributions and payouts
  • Assign every transfer a unique reference so it can be traced
  • Verify the receipt a member uploads before the admin confirms
  • Show all members a shared view of the circle's progress

✗ We do not

  • Hold or move your money — funds stay in your branch's bank
  • Lend or invest — no credit is extended, no returns guaranteed
  • Replace your branch's own rules — each branch sets its own
Glossary

Terminology

Every term you'll see on the platform. Expand any term for a plain-English definition.

ROSCA

Rotating Savings and Credit Association. A savings tradition where a group of people contribute a fixed amount at regular intervals, and each period one member receives the whole pool.

Branch

A local group running its own circles — typically a church branch or community organisation. Each branch has its own bank account, admin, and members.

Circle (Tenant)

One group of exactly 12 members saving together. A branch can run multiple circles. We use "tenant" internally, "circle" on most screens.

Slot

Your position in the circle, 1 to 12. Slots are assigned randomly at formation. Slot 1 receives in cycle 1, slot 12 in cycle 12.

Cycle

One month of the circle's run. Cycle 1 is the first month, cycle 12 the last. Each cycle, one member receives the pool.

Tenure

A full 12-cycle run — one year. When it ends, members vote on whether to renew for another year with the same slots.

Contribution

The fixed amount each member pays every cycle. On this platform typically £1,000/month, though your branch may set a different amount.

Pool (Pot)

The total collected from all 12 members in one cycle. For £1,000 contributions, the pool is £12,000.

Payout

The amount paid to the cycle's recipient. Pool, adjusted for TVC, minus the platform fee.

TVC

Time-Value Contribution. A small adjustment so later receivers are compensated for time their money spent in the pool. 2% per year, scaled to slot position.

Platform fee

1.5% of the post-TVC pool, deducted at payout time. Retained by Thrifty Technologies for running the ledger and support.

Reference

Unique code on every transfer. C7-P3-M3 for contributions, C7-P3-PAYOUT for payouts.

Receipt

Photo or screenshot of a bank transfer. Members upload them for contributions, admins for payouts.

KYC

Know Your Customer. Identity verification — full legal name, home address, bank details — required before joining a circle.

Ledger

Append-only record of every contribution, payout, and fee. Cannot be edited or deleted by anyone.

Renewal vote

When a tenure ends, every member votes on continuing another year. All 12 must agree to renew.

Branch admin

The person at your branch who runs the circle. Verifies contributions, confirms payouts, handles issues.

Platform operator

Thrifty Technologies — provides the ledger, storage, notifications, support. Does not hold or move money.
