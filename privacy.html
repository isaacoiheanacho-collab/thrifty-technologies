Privacy Policy

How Thrifty Technologies handles personal data as the platform operator.

Effective date: [Insert date]  |  Last updated: [Insert date]  |  Version: 1.0

1. Introduction

Thrifty Technologies ("Thrifty", "we", "us" or "our") operates the ROSCA Thrift & Savings platform ("the Platform").

We respect the privacy of individuals who access or use the Platform, including Community Partners, Community Admins and Co-Savers.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected, how it is used, when it may be shared, how it is protected and what rights individuals may have in relation to their information.

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, protect and improve the Platform.

This Policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

2. Important information about the Platform

The Platform provides digital tools that enable communities to administer their own rotating savings arrangements, commonly known as ROSCA, Esusu or Ajo.

Thrifty provides the technology and administrative facilities. It does not organise or independently operate the savings arrangements established by individual communities.

Community Partners and their authorised Community Admins are responsible for administering their own Circles, maintaining their agreed Circle Rules, overseeing contributions and arranging payouts.

Contributions and payouts are handled directly between Co-Savers and the designated bank account or other agreed payment arrangement controlled by the relevant Community Partner or its authorised Admin.

Thrifty does not receive, collect, hold, safeguard, pool, invest or distribute Co-Savers' contributions or Circle payout funds.

The Platform may record information about contributions, collection positions and payouts for administrative and record-keeping purposes. Recording or displaying this information does not mean that Thrifty receives or controls the underlying money.

3. Who is responsible for personal data?

The responsibility for personal data depends on the purpose for which it is being processed.

3.1 Thrifty Technologies

Thrifty is responsible for personal data it processes for its own platform-related purposes, including:

  • Creating and administering Platform accounts.
  • Managing Community Partner registration.
  • Providing login and authentication facilities.
  • Maintaining Platform security and preventing misuse.
  • Providing technical support and responding to enquiries.
  • Managing its own business administration and service communications.
  • Meeting legal obligations that apply directly to the Platform operator.

For these activities, Thrifty may act as a data controller under applicable data protection legislation.

3.2 Community Partners and Community Admins

A Community Partner may determine how personal information relating to its community and savings Circles is collected and used.

For example, a Community Partner may decide what membership information is required, how contributions are recorded, who may access Circle records and how long its own records should be retained.

Depending on the actual arrangements, the Community Partner or the individual responsible for its administration may be a data controller for these activities.

3.3 Thrifty acting on behalf of a Community Partner

Where Thrifty processes personal information solely to provide the Platform's Circle administration facilities on behalf of a Community Partner, and in accordance with that Partner's instructions, Thrifty may act as a data processor.

The precise responsibilities of Thrifty and the relevant Community Partner depend on the actual processing activities and arrangements between them.

Nothing in this Policy changes the legal responsibilities that apply to either party under applicable data protection law.

4. Personal information we may collect

The information collected depends on how an individual uses the Platform and what information is necessary for the relevant service.

4.1 Account and identity information

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Display name.
  • Username or account identifier.
  • Telephone number.
  • Email address.
  • Date of birth or age confirmation, where required.
  • Account registration and verification status.
4.2 Community and Circle information

Where an individual participates in a Circle, the Platform may process information such as:

  • Community Partner or Circle name.
  • Circle membership and participant details.
  • Assigned collection position or slot.
  • Agreed contribution amount and schedule.
  • Contribution status and relevant dates.
  • Payout position and administrative payout records.
  • Circle participation and account status.
  • Information entered by an authorised Community Admin.
4.3 Transaction and administrative records

The Platform may store information entered by a Community Admin or other authorised user to maintain Circle records, including:

  • Whether a contribution has been reported as received.
  • Contribution dates and amounts.
  • Payout calculations and collection positions.
  • Relevant administrative notes.
  • Transfer references or confirmation details, where supplied.

These are administrative records. They do not mean that Thrifty receives, processes or controls the underlying contribution or payout funds.

Thrifty does not require access to a Co-Saver's online banking password, bank login credentials or payment-card security details to provide the basic Circle record-keeping service.

4.4 Technical and device information

When individuals access the Platform, technical information may be generated or collected, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Login and session information.
  • Access dates and times.
  • Security and diagnostic logs.
4.5 Communications and support information

Where an individual contacts us, we may retain:

  • The individual's contact details.
  • The contents of their enquiry.
  • Relevant correspondence.
  • Information needed to investigate and respond to the enquiry.
4.6 Information we do not routinely require

The Platform's ordinary Circle administration service does not require Thrifty to collect or hold Co-Savers' bank account funds, bank login credentials, payment-card details, passport copies or other identity documents.

If a particular feature or legal requirement makes additional information necessary, we will explain the information required and the reason for collecting it before or when it is collected.

We will not claim to carry out identity verification, financial suitability assessments or anti-money-laundering checks unless those activities are actually undertaken and there is an applicable legal basis for doing so.

5. Why we use personal information

We use personal information only for purposes connected with operating, administering, securing and supporting the Platform, or for other purposes permitted by law.

These purposes may include:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing access to the relevant community or Circle.
  • Enabling authorised users to administer Circle records.
  • Displaying contribution and payout information.
  • Supporting the calculation of administrative Circle figures.
  • Sending essential account and service notifications.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Maintaining system security and preventing unauthorised access.
  • Identifying technical problems and improving Platform performance.
  • Maintaining appropriate business and service records.
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.
  • Complying with legal obligations that apply to us.

We do not use Co-Savers' contributions or payouts as funds belonging to Thrifty.

6. Our lawful bases for processing

Where UK data protection law applies, we identify an appropriate lawful basis for each processing activity.

Depending on the activity, these may include:

6.1 Contract

Where processing is necessary to provide an account, operate the Platform or perform the service arrangements applicable to an individual.

6.2 Legitimate interests

Where processing is reasonably necessary for legitimate interests pursued by Thrifty or another relevant party, provided those interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and freedoms.

Examples may include maintaining Platform security, preventing misuse, providing technical support and protecting the integrity of administrative records.

6.3 Legal obligation

Where we are required to process or retain information to comply with a legal obligation that applies to us.

6.4 Consent

Where we rely on consent for a particular activity, we will request it appropriately and explain the purpose.

An individual may withdraw consent at any time where consent is the lawful basis for that processing. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

We will not treat consent as the default lawful basis for every activity.

7. Who may access or receive personal information?

Personal information may be accessed or shared only where reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and subject to appropriate safeguards.

7.1 Community Partners and authorised Community Admins

An authorised Community Admin may access the information reasonably required to administer the relevant community or Circle.

This may include participant names, membership information, collection positions, contribution records and payout administration information.

The scope of access should be limited to the responsibilities assigned to that Admin.

7.2 Other Co-Savers

Other participants in a Circle may be shown limited information needed to understand the operation of their Circle.

Depending on the Circle's settings and agreed arrangements, this may include:

  • Display names.
  • Collection positions.
  • Contribution status.
  • Relevant Circle schedules and administrative information.

A participant should not automatically have access to another participant's private contact details, identification information or unrelated account information.

7.3 Technical service providers

We may use third-party providers to support services such as:

  • Website and application hosting.
  • Database and cloud infrastructure.
  • Security and monitoring.
  • Email and SMS delivery.
  • Technical maintenance and support.

Where a provider processes personal information on our behalf, we will seek to ensure that appropriate contractual and security arrangements are in place.

7.4 Legal and regulatory disclosures

We may disclose information where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where disclosure is otherwise legally permitted and reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety or security.

7.5 No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

We do not disclose personal information to unrelated organisations for their independent marketing purposes.

8. Community Partner responsibilities

Community Partners and their authorised Admins must handle personal information obtained through their use of the Platform responsibly.

They should:

  • Collect only information reasonably necessary for their community's activities.
  • Explain to Co-Savers how their information will be used.
  • Restrict access to authorised individuals.
  • Avoid sharing private member information unnecessarily.
  • Keep their own records accurate.
  • Protect information outside the Platform, including bank statements and other records they control.
  • Respond appropriately to privacy enquiries relating to their own activities.
  • Follow applicable data protection requirements.

A Community Partner must not assume that using the Platform transfers all responsibility for its own collection, use or disclosure of personal information to Thrifty.

9. How long we retain personal information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ according to the type of information and the reason it is held.

For example:

  • Account information may be retained while an account remains active and for a reasonable period after closure.
  • Circle administration records may be retained for the period necessary to complete administration, resolve outstanding issues and meet applicable record-keeping requirements.
  • Security logs may be retained for a period appropriate to security monitoring and investigation.
  • Support correspondence may be retained for as long as necessary to manage the enquiry and any related dispute.
  • Information required for legal claims or legal compliance may be retained for the applicable period.

Where information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it or securely dispose of it.

Some information may need to be retained after an account is closed where a legal obligation applies or where it is reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

We do not promise that all information will automatically be deleted immediately when a user closes an account or leaves a Circle.

10. International access and transfers

The Platform may be used by eligible communities and individuals located in different countries, subject to applicable laws and any restrictions we may impose.

Personal information may therefore be accessed or processed in countries outside the United Kingdom, depending on the location of our hosting infrastructure, technical service providers and users.

Where UK data protection law requires safeguards for a transfer of personal information outside the United Kingdom, we will take the steps required by law. These may include relying on an applicable adequacy regulation or using an appropriate transfer safeguard.

Where required, information about the applicable safeguards will be made available on request.

11. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, destruction, alteration or disclosure.

Depending on the service and the information involved, these measures may include:

  • Access controls and account authentication.
  • Restrictions on administrative permissions.
  • Secure communication arrangements.
  • Appropriate hosting and infrastructure safeguards.
  • System monitoring and security logs.
  • Backups and recovery procedures.
  • Procedures for managing suspected security incidents.

No website, application, database or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users must also protect their login credentials, avoid sharing passwords and notify the relevant support contact if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

12. Personal data breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach involving information for which we have responsibility, we will assess the incident and take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

Where notification to the Information Commissioner's Office or affected individuals is legally required, we will make the necessary notification within the applicable time limits.

Where Thrifty acts as a processor for a Community Partner, we will notify the relevant controller of a breach in accordance with our contractual and legal responsibilities.

13. Your data protection rights

Where UK data protection law applies, individuals may have the right to:

  • Access: request confirmation of whether we process their personal information and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: request deletion of personal information in circumstances where the law provides that right.
  • Restriction: request that the use of personal information be restricted in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability: request certain personal information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, where the right applies.
  • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests or to processing for direct marketing, where the relevant right applies.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent where we rely on consent as the lawful basis for processing.
  • Automated decision-making: obtain the protections provided by law where a decision is made solely by automated processing and has the relevant legal or similarly significant effect.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. They do not apply in exactly the same way to every type of processing.

For example, we may be permitted or required to retain certain records despite receiving a deletion request.

14. Requests concerning Circle information

Some personal information relates to the administration of a particular community or Circle rather than to Thrifty's own independent purposes.

Where a request concerns information controlled by a Community Partner, we may direct the individual to the relevant Community Partner or Admin.

Where Thrifty is acting as a processor, we will assist the relevant controller as required by our contractual and legal responsibilities.

Where the request concerns information for which Thrifty is itself the controller, we will consider and respond to the request in accordance with applicable law.

Closing an account or leaving a Circle does not automatically remove records that must reasonably be retained for outstanding administration, legal compliance or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

15. Children and eligibility

The Platform is intended for individuals aged 18 or over.

We do not knowingly invite individuals under the age of 18 to register for or participate in the Platform.

If we become aware that personal information has been submitted by an individual under 18, we will assess the circumstances and take appropriate steps, which may include restricting access and deleting the information where appropriate.

16. Cookies and similar technologies

The Platform and its website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, account sessions, security and user preferences.

Where non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, we will obtain consent where required by applicable law.

Individuals may be able to manage cookie preferences through their browser or any cookie preference facility provided on the website.

Further information may be provided in a separate Cookie Policy or cookie notice.

17. Automated processing

The Platform may use automated processes to perform administrative calculations, display Circle information, manage account access and support security.

These processes do not, merely by being automated, mean that Thrifty makes decisions about an individual's eligibility for financial products, creditworthiness or access to banking services.

Where a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects is introduced, we will provide the information and safeguards required by applicable law.

18. External websites and third-party services

The Platform may contain links to external websites or services operated by third parties.

We do not control the privacy practices of those external services. Individuals should review the privacy policies and terms applicable to any third-party service they choose to use.

Where a Community Partner uses its own bank, payment provider or other external service to administer contributions or payouts, the privacy practices of that provider are governed by its own terms and privacy information.

19. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Platform, our data processing activities, applicable law or operational requirements.

Where a change is material, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to users' attention, such as displaying a notice on the Platform or providing an appropriate notification.

The updated version will state its effective date and version number.

Continued use of the Platform after an updated policy takes effect does not replace any consent that must separately be obtained by law.

If an individual believes that their personal information has been handled improperly, they may contact us first so that we can review and respond to their concerns.

Individuals also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

Website: https://ico.org.uk/

Making a complaint to us first is encouraged but is not a condition for contacting the ICO.
