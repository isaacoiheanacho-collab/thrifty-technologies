Platform Advantage

Why Community Partners Trust Us

Engineered specifically to solve traditional ledger disputes and cash-handling bottlenecks.

Bank-in-Control Architecture

Funds remain directly within your community or partner bank accounts. Thrifty Technologies never touches or holds your capital; we provide the immutable ledger and reconciliation layer.

Append-Only Digital Ledger

Every contribution and payout is permanently recorded with unique reference tags, eliminating lost WhatsApp receipts and manual disputes.

Automatic TVC Adjustment

Our built-in 2% per annum Time-Value Collection (TVC) automatically computes fairness premiums so early pool collectors fairly balance value with late collectors.
