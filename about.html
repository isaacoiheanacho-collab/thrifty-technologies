About

About the platform

ROSCA Thrift brings traditional Esusu savings circles into the digital age—connecting trusted communities, from churches and town associations to social media groups. Built around the way you already save together, our platform brings transparency, security, and automation to contributions and payouts, making community-led financial services simpler and more accessible.

Who runs this

Infrastructure & Accountability

The platform is operated by Thrifty Technologies, a United Kingdom entity. We provide the software, the servers, the ledger, and the support.

The community partners — individual churches, social groups or community organizations — are the ones running the actual savings circles. Each partner has its own bank account, its own pool of members, its own terms of service, privacy policy and its own admin who verifies contributions and payouts.

Platform Boundaries

What the platform does — and does not do

✓ We do

  • Record each member's contributions and payouts
  • Assign every transfer a unique reference so it can be traced
  • Verify the receipt a member uploads before the admin confirms
  • Show all members a shared view of the circle's progress

✗ We do not

  • Hold or move your money — funds stay in your partner's bank
  • Lend or invest — no credit is extended, no returns guaranteed
  • Replace your partner's own rules — each community sets its own
Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Driven by financial inclusion and community empowerment.

Vision / Mission positioning

Traditional Trust & Digital Solutions

To bring every community savings circle into the digital age—replacing paper records with a single, trusted source of truth in every member’s palm, where contributions are visible, records are clear, and trusted groups can grow beyond geographical boundaries, regardless of their background or credit history.

Service commitment / Value proposition

Reliability & Transparency

To provide reliable, transparent, and secure digital solutions by digitising traditional thrift, credit and cooperative savings practices. We equip trusted communities and social groups with simple digital tools to manage their Esusu savings circles—automating record-keeping, tracking contributions and payouts, and strengthening accountability through one shared platform.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

The foundational principles that guide every feature we build and every community we support.

Integrity

We uphold honesty and ethical conduct in everything we do. From how savings arrangements are administered to how information is presented, we believe doing what is right should always come first.

Shared Responsibility

We believe successful savings communities depend on everyone playing their part. We encourage community partners and members to honour their commitments and contribute towards shared financial goals.

Financial Discipline

We promote consistent saving habits and responsible financial commitments. Our technology helps communities establish structured savings routines and stay focused on their collective goals.

Simplicity

We believe managing savings should never be complicated. We transform traditional paperwork and manual processes into straightforward digital experiences that anyone can navigate.

Fairness & Equity

We believe every member deserves fair treatment, regardless of their background or financial standing. We support equitable savings arrangements through clearly defined contributions, payout schedules, and financial obligations.

Innovation

We build solutions that help savings communities grow and thrive over time. By combining traditional collective saving practices with adaptable technology, we create opportunities for stronger and more resilient communities.

Important Notice

Understanding Our Service

ROSCA Thrift & Savings is a facilitation platform that enables individuals to participate in traditional Rotating Savings and Credit Association (ROSCA) groups.

We do not offer investment services or act as a bank. We do not hold, invest, or manage funds beyond facilitating ROSCA group contributions.

Participation is voluntary and based on mutual trust among verified group members. We provide the technology, security, and administrative tools to make these cooperative savings arrangements more transparent and efficient.
